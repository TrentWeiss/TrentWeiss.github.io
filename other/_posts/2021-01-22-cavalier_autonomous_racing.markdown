I am also a member of Cavalier Autonomous Racing, University of Virginia's entry into the Indy Autonomous Challenge, the world's premier autonomous racing league. 

Famed American racer Mario Andretti once said "If everything seems under control, then you are not going fast enough".  My work in Cavalier Autonomous designing various autonomy subsystems for the AV21 is guided by this philosophy.
