Trent Weiss

I am a PhD Candidate in Computer Science at the University of Virginia Link Lab, working with Professor Madhur Behl. My undergraduate studies were in Mechinical Engineering with a focus on Mechatronics. Before joining the Link Lab in 2017, I was a robotics engineer with the Research & Technology division of The Boeing Company.

My research interests are in Robotics and Autonomous/Cyber-Physical Systems. Specifically, I am interested in improving autonomous vehicles by taking them to their extreme limit in autonomous racing scenarios.
